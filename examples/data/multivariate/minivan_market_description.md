## Minivan market (six competing minivans)

### Description

Six minivans competing in one market, for simulating market shares with a choice model estimated on the `minivan` data (Multivariate > Conjoint > Choice conjoint > Predict > Dataset). Without a choice task column all rows form one choice set. The designs are those in the share simulation of the minivan example in Chapman and Feit, *R for Marketing Research and Analytics* (Springer), chapter 13.

### Variables

- product = Product number (1-6); product 1 is the new design being evaluated
- seat = Number of seats (6, 7, 8)
- cargo = Cargo space (2ft, 3ft)
- eng = Engine (gas, hyb = hybrid, elec = electric)
- price = Price in $1,000 (30, 35, 40)
