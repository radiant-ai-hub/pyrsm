    The drop appears OVB-safe based on the statistical check.

HOWEVER: with n=200 and only 3 predictors, there is no real reason
to simplify in the first place. Recommendation: KEEP Age in the
reported model. Non-significance does not mean 'no effect' - it
means we lack evidence in this sample. The OVB-safe finding is a
pedagogical confirmation, not a license to drop.
