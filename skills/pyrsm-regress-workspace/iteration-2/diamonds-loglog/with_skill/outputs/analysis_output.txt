Interpretation example:
  clarity[IF] beta = 1.0804
    -> exp(1.0804) - 1 = 1.9460
    -> 194.60% higher price than an I1-clarity diamond,
       holding ln(carat) (i.e. carat) constant.

Carat elasticity (log-log):
  beta on ln(carat) = 1.8091
  Interpretation: a 1% increase in carat is associated with 
  approximately a 1.809% increase in price, holding clarity constant.
  Equivalently, doubling carat multiplies price by 2^1.809 = 3.504.
