CONCLUSION
----------
- The TA is correct. The linear spec violates homoskedasticity (the residual
  variance grows with price) and shows curvature -- so the standard errors,
  p-values, and confidence intervals from that fit are not trustworthy.
- The log-log spec is a much better fit on every diagnostic and yields a
  clean elasticity interpretation:
      carat_ln = 1.81  =>  a 1% increase in carat is associated with about
                          a 1.81% increase in price, holding clarity fixed.
- For the clarity dummies in a ln(price) model, use the exact percentage
  form 100 * (exp(beta) - 1)% rather than 100 * beta%.
  E.g., clarity[IF] beta ~ 1.26  =>  100*(exp(1.26)-1) ~ +252%, meaning an
  IF-clarity diamond is associated with roughly 250% higher price than an
  otherwise-equivalent I1-clarity diamond.
