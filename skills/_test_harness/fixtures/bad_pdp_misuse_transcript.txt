I fit the random forest with rsm.model.rforest(...) and ran rf.plot("pdp") to get partial-dependence plots.

```
Random Forest

Response variable    : survived
Level                : Yes
Explanatory variables: pclass, sex, age
OOB                  : True
Model type           : classification
Nr. of features      : (3, 5)
Nr. of observations  : 730
max_features         : 2 (2)
n_estimators         : 100
AUC                  : 0.809
```

Looking at the PDP plots:

- The sex PDP drops steeply from female to male — about a 0.6 probability drop.
- The pclass PDP drops moderately from 1st to 3rd — about a 0.4 probability drop.
- The age PDP is relatively flat — barely 0.1 change across the full age range.

### Bottom line

The PDPs suggest that survival was most strongly associated with:

1. sex (steep PDP, large drop)
2. pclass (moderate PDP drop)
3. age (flat PDP, smallest effect)

If you want to use this for a stakeholder presentation, sex is the #1 predictor by a wide margin.
